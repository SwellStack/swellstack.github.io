Privacy Policy — last updated September 5, 2026

Scope of this policy

This policy covers the identity provider we call Authentication-api and the "Sign in with Google" flow it exposes, used by SwellStack's internal tools (MyVault, LineEdge, and the backoffices used to administer them). These are internal tools we build and run for our own use, not a public consumer product — this policy exists so that, whichever of them you use, the same rules apply to your account data.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Google shares with us:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google account profile picture (if you have one)
  • A Google-issued identifier for your account (not your Google password — we never see it)

If you register with an email and password instead of Google, we collect and store your name, email address, and a salted hash of your password — never the password itself in plain text.

Once you're signed in, the specific tool you use (MyVault, LineEdge, etc.) stores the data you enter into that tool — for example, MyVault stores the financial records you add, LineEdge stores the picks and parlays it evaluates for you. That data belongs to the tool you're using and is scoped to your account only; it is not shared between tools or with the other sections of SwellStack.

Why we collect it

We use this data strictly to:

  • Create and identify your account across sessions.
  • Authenticate you and enforce which tool(s) and permissions your account has access to.
  • Contact you about your account if needed (e.g., a security notice).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with any third party other than Google (solely to verify your sign-in) and the infrastructure providers that host our services (database and hosting providers), who process it only on our behalf and under contract, never for their own purposes.

Where it's stored

Account and authentication data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database with encryption at rest, accessible only by the SwellStack services that need it. Access tokens are short-lived (about 15 minutes); refresh tokens are stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text, so a database leak alone would not expose usable credentials.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing swellstack@gmail.com from the address registered to the account — we'll confirm and process the deletion within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

SwellStack's tools are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of any SwellStack tool after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: swellstack@gmail.com.
