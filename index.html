SwellStack

A small suite of internal tools, one shared identity provider.

What SwellStack is

SwellStack is not a consumer product — it's the umbrella under which we build and operate a handful of internal tools for our own use, sharing one central identity and access-control service (Authentication-api) instead of each tool re-implementing its own login, users, and permissions.

Signing in with Google against SwellStack authenticates you into one of these tools:

  • MyVault — a personal finance tracker.
  • LineEdge — a sports-prediction and betting-analysis platform.
  • Internal backoffices used to administer the apps above (managing accounts, API keys, and permissions).

Data and privacy

When you sign in with Google, we ask for your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture) to create and identify your account. Read exactly what we collect and why in our Privacy Policy.
